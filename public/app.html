What the app does

    Mobile check-in

    Check in at the gate, not the desk

    Your room key appears in the app 24 hours before arrival. At the gate, hold your phone to the reader, the door opens, and that is the whole interaction.

    Express checkout closes the folio the night before, so departure is a receipt in your inbox rather than a queue at 11am on a Sunday. If anything on the bill is wrong you can dispute it from the app while you are still in the lift.

    1. 24 hours before arrival

      Digital key is issued, check-in opens, and the folio is already itemised.

    2. At the gate

      Phone to reader, door opens, no queue, no printed key card.

    3. The night before departure

      Express checkout. Review, dispute if needed, settle in the app.

      Pay your way

      Cash, card, or points — mixed freely

      Points for the room, cash for the dinner, the rest on the card you already have. The app splits a single stay across payment methods however you like, and shows the split before you commit — no calling a hotel to arrange it.

      Paying with points never costs more than the cash rate. A 25,000-point night is a 25,000-point night, and the rate parity guarantee applies to it too.

      • 25,000 points for a free night at any Ultra property
      • 5,000 points for a dining credit at any restaurant in the group
      • 40,000 points for a confirmed suite upgrade
      • Points never expire while your membership is active

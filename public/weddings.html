What you are not billed for

Every Ultra wedding package includes the ceremony setting, the dinner, a dedicated coordinator, and the tables, linen and glassware. Those are not line items, because a wedding quote that itemises the chairs is a warning sign.

The things that are genuinely extra — florals, live music, a second day's catering, a full property buy-out — are quoted up front as fixed prices before you sign anything.

  • A named wedding coordinator from enquiry to the last dance
  • Guest-room blocks at the property, never marked up
  • Ultra Points earned on the accommodation spend, at the same rate as a stay
  • Airport transfer desk for your guests on request
  • Rain plan for every outdoor ceremony, agreed in writing beforehand
  • Photography and styling partners held at group rates, or yours if you prefer

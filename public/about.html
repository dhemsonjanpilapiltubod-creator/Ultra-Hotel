Sustainability

Measured, not promised

We publish Scope 1 and 2 emissions per property every year, including the properties where the number went up. We set a procurement target of 70% regional by 2030 and report what we actually hit. Rainwater harvesting, greywater reuse and reef-safe laundry chemistry are standard at every resort rather than upgrades we sell.

Nineteen of our resort properties sit on or beside a protected reef, and each funds its local management partner directly. If a commitment cannot be measured at property level, it does not go on this page.

    Owners & operators

    Managing an Ultra property

    We work with a small number of operating partners and we take a minority position in the buildings. If you have a hotel that fits the group — a landmark, a historic building, somewhere with a reason to exist — the first conversation is about the building, not the number.

    Long leases and management agreements are our preference. We are not interested in short-term management contracts or rapid asset-stripping.

    What we look for

    • A building with real character and a reason to protect it
    • Guest rooms — at least forty, and we will discuss more
    • Full-service operation with F&B and a spa or equivalent
    • Freehold or a lease with at least twenty-five years to run

    Accessibility

    Everyone should be able to get through the door

    Step-free access to every public area, accessible guest rooms on every floor that has them, and roll-in showers and grab rails in every Ultra Hotel and Aether property. Pulse properties are held to the same standard.

    If a room you need is not available on the dates you want, tell us in advance and we will either find one that is or tell you honestly which properties can offer it. If a property cannot accommodate a specific need, we would rather you knew before you booked than on arrival.

    • Step-free routes from car park to lobby, restaurant, spa and pool
    • Accessible rooms with roll-in showers, lowered sinks and visual alarms
    • Service animals welcome at every property in the group, without exception
    • Braille and tactile signage at reception in all Ultra Hotels
    • Lift announcements in every lift; hearing loop at every front desk

    Need something specific that is not listed here? Email access@ultrahotel.com and a person will reply, usually the same day.
